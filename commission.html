THE PROCESS

How It Works

  1. Send your request. Tell me about your project, goals, and requirements.
  2. Discuss the details. We clarify the scope, deliverables, budget, and deadline.
  3. Confirm the agreement. Once the project details, price, and deadline are agreed upon, a 50% upfront deposit is required before work begins. The remaining 50% is due upon completion, before the final files are delivered.
  4. Review and delivery. The project timeline depends on the workload and complexity of the project. I will complete the agreed work, provide the included revisions, and deliver the final files once the remaining balance has been paid.

PLEASE READ

Terms & Information

  • Payment: A 50% deposit is required before work begins. The remaining 50% must be paid upon completion, before the final files are delivered.
  • Project Timeline: The completion time depends on the workload, complexity, and requirements of each project. An estimated deadline will be discussed before work begins.
  • Revisions: Up to 3 revisions are included for each commission. Additional revisions will cost an extra 20 THB per revision. However, programming and game development commissions include unlimited revisions.
  • Cancellations & Refunds: Once work has begun, all payments are non-refundable. If a commission is cancelled after work has started, the initial 50% deposit will be retained and will not be refunded.
  • Project Scope: Please provide clear requirements, references, and necessary files before work begins. Requests that significantly change the agreed scope may require additional fees and an updated deadline.
  • Usage Rights: Commissions intended for commercial use will be charged at twice the standard rate (2× the original price). Except Video Editing which already included the price. Clear and appropriate credit must be given to the creator whenever the work is used or published.
Important Notice
Please make sure you understand and agree to these terms before placing a commission. Work will begin only after the project details have been confirmed and the 50% deposit has been received.
