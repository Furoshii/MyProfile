THE PROCESS

How It Works

  1. Send your request. Tell me about your project, goals, and requirements.
  2. Discuss the details. We clarify the scope, deliverables, budget, and deadline.
  3. Confirm the agreement. Work begins after the scope, price, and payment terms have been agreed upon.
  4. Review and delivery. I deliver the agreed work and handle any revisions included in our agreement.

PLEASE READ

Terms & Information

  • Prices are confirmed individually before work begins.
  • Deadlines depend on the project's scope and my availability.
  • Revision limits and additional revision fees will be agreed upon in advance.
  • Payment methods and payment schedules will be discussed before work begins.
  • Commercial usage rights and portfolio display permissions must be agreed upon for each project.
  • Please provide all necessary references, files, and requirements before work begins.
By contacting me, you are not automatically booking a commission. A commission is confirmed only after both parties agree on the project details and terms.
